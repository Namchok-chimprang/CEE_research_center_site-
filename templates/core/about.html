About Overview

Who We Are

The Center of Excellence in Econometrics is a research center at Chiang Mai University focused on advanced econometric knowledge, quantitative economics, AI-integrated methods, and evidence-based policy solutions. The center works with domestic and international partners to produce high-quality research, develop analytical tools, transfer knowledge, and support policy decisions for sustainable development.

Background

A research center at Chiang Mai University focused on advanced econometric tools, interdisciplinary collaboration, and evidence-based policy solutions for economic, social, and sustainable development.

Founded in 2013

Mission

Lead frontier econometric knowledge, develop practical analytical tools, support high-quality research, and translate quantitative evidence into policy and societal impact.

Objectives

Advance econometric innovation, integrate research across disciplines and SDGs, and strengthen knowledge transfer through training, consulting, and academic services.
